### design.md Decisions list does not include the two new disciplines added in iter 5

**Description**: The Decisions section enumerates 12 decisions (D1–D12). The Risks list at line 154 mentions pushback discipline ("Text duplication across SKILL.md files for pushback discipline"), but neither of the two new disciplines added during iter 5 — **change completeness** and **read-before-reference** — appears as a design decision. The README's Cross-cutting disciplines section (line 188) treats all three as a coherent package, and the orbit-conventions spec has substantial new requirements codifying them. design.md presents itself as the authoritative summary of "the key technical decisions for implementers" (line 11), so leaving the two new disciplines invisible there means an implementer reading only design.md would miss two of the three execution-time disciplines they're expected to bake into SKILL.md content. (Optional alternative: extend D6's rationale on pushback discipline to acknowledge that change completeness and read-before-reference are the other two members of the bracket.)
